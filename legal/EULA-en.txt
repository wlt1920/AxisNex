2. WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES TO YOUR SYSTEM
To work, the Software makes changes to your Windows system. By installing it you agree to these changes:
a) It installs the HIDMaestro user-mode driver, which creates a virtual DualSense controller. HIDMaestro installs a self-signed driver certificate named "HIDMaestroTestCert" into the "Trusted Root Certification Authorities" and "Trusted Publishers" stores of your computer so that its driver can load.
b) It installs HidHide (by Nefarius Software Solutions), a filter driver that hides your physical controller from games while the Software is running. HidHide only becomes active after a restart.
c) It registers itself in HidHide's list of allowed applications, raises its own process priority and Windows timer resolution while running, and stores settings and profiles in %APPDATA%\Input Zero and HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Input Zero.
Uninstalling Input Zero removes the application. HidHide, the HIDMaestro driver and its certificate stay installed and can be removed separately from Windows Settings / Device Manager / Certificate Manager (certlm.msc).
